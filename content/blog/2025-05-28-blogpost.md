      Everyone’s hyped about LLMOps. But here’s the truth: it’s just MLOps—on hard mode.
      <br><br>

* **Versioning?** Harder. You’re not just tracking datasets—you’re managing billion-parameter checkpoints.

* **Observability?** Trickier. Hallucinations don’t show up in accuracy metrics.

* **Drift?** Way more subtle. Prompt drift is a new beast.

* **Deployment?** Good luck. You need GPU orchestration, not just container scaling.

    </div>
    <div class="col-md-4">

* **Evaluation?** Traditional metrics (accuracy, precision) don’t cut it. You need human feedback loops.

* **Governance?** The risks are higher. One wrong output can lead to brand damage or legal issues.

LLMOps isn’t just MLOps. It’s MLOps+Regulations+UX+Latency+Explainability+PromptOps.
