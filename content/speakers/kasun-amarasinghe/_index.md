As a Research Scientist working in the intersection of AI/ML and Public policy, I study how data science and AI systems can support public policy decisions to improve the efficiency and equity of public service delivery. I study three distinct but connected areas: 
1. Building and deploying AI/ML systems in partnership with government agencies and non-profit organizations across various policy domains.
2. Conducting methodological research in responsible AI, with emphasis on explainability and algorithmic fairness.
3. Developing and leading training programs for students, data scientists, and policy practitioners. These programs teach participants how to scope, develop, and evaluate AI systems for public policy applications.
