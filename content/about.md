### What kind of content can I expect?

The overall purpose of Abracadata is to provide insight into the people, practices and tools that are involved in providing the data that fuels AI solutions. 

The program will cover:

 * Data acquisition & ingestion
 * Databases & storage
 * Data transformation & processing
 * Data versioning, observability & lineage
 * MLOps & LLMOps
 * Data people (roles, teams, culture)


### Who is this event for?

 * __Data Sorcerers (Data Engineers & MLOps Wizards)__ – Masters of pipelines and spellbinding architectures, crafting scalable and resilient data flows.
 * __Keepers of the Data Grimoire (Data Platform Managers)__ – Guardians of powerful platforms, ensuring data magic runs smoothly and efficiently.
 * __Guild Masters of Engineering (Engineering Managers in Data Realms)__ – Visionary leaders orchestrating teams of data enchanters to conjure innovation and business success.
